Okay, what?

I'll answer all your questions to the best of my ability.

What's this?

This is a ToxiPlays project aiming to merge music with gambling.

Why?

Boredom and the thought that someone might spend their last dime on bullshit.

How?

Buy a pull, click on the blurry song titles, click again to listen to 'em. It's that bloody simple.

What's the rarities mean?

Glad you asked! There's an actual system behind this.

  • Common tracks have already been released to the public and are available elsewhere right now.
  • Uncommon tracks are unheard variants of songs that are released. Think: covers, remixes, demos, etc.
  • Rare tracks have been taken down from public platforms. If you pull them, you'll get them again in original quality.
  • Legendary tracks have never released before—they are exclusively available via a lucky Loottox pull.

What's the catch?

You can stream the songs you pull through here... and that's it. But I did try my hardest to make it a pleasant streaming experience! You can download three songs for offline listening for one hour after purchase. Download rights are not granted for a Basic pull, because I need to make money somehow.

And the legality of it all?

You are not permitted to reupload songs that Loottox grants you access to, or to otherwise exploit them, commercially or not. Okay, but what about my sensitive payment info?

Right. Loottox process payments with Stripe. You'll want to consult their Privacy Policy. All in all, I don't get any crazy information from you or from Stripe. All Stripe gives us is an OK to authorize Standard/Ultra pulls. Truuuuust me...

What's your return/refund policy?

I can't easily un-give you access to the song. Once you've heard it, you've heard it. Thereby, all sales are final. No refunds will be issued, unless you can prove that a replicatable issue with the Software prevented you from getting your money's worth. If that's the case, contact me.
